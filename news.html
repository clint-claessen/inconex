Student Assistant wanted

The ERC-funded project INCONEX, led by Dr Lucy Kinski, sought a motivated student assistant to join the team at the University of Salzburg — supporting literature and data research, data processing, and content analysis (10 hours/week; BA/MA enrolment; very good German and/or Spanish).

12 December 2025

Kick-off Workshop — Rethinking Representation Through Absence

The INCONEX kick-off workshop brought together leading experts to discuss representative claims, group appeals, and the crucial role of absence in representation. Held online on Friday, 12 December 2025, 13:45–17:45 CET.

October 2025

Welcome to our new PhDs!

We were delighted to welcome two new PhD students, Anna-Lotta Dechow and Oscar Mendoza Hernandez, to the INCONEX team. Over the coming years, we will together investigate how citizens and interests become absent from democratic representation — whether intentionally or unintentionally.

ECPR General Conference 2025

Project team shares first results in Thessaloniki

At the ECPR General Conference 2025, Lucy Kinski and Clint Claessen presented two papers — on conceptualizing representative absence, and on using text-as-data methods to capture the presence and absence of social groups in parliamentary debates.
